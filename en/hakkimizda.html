About Us

GEK Hukuk Law Office was founded in Adana in 2014 by attorney Tuğsan Güneş. Today, attorneys Tuğsan Güneş, Ömer Murat Erdil and Adem Karakaya work together at the firm. The firm's name comes from the initials of our lawyers' surnames.

We provide advisory and litigation services to individuals and legal entities, mainly in corporate, commercial, real estate, employment, family and inheritance law. We work in Turkish and English in both advisory and litigation matters.

Our working principles

  • We explain clearly to our clients their legal situation, the options available and the consequences of each option.
  • We keep our clients regularly informed about the progress of their matter.
  • In accordance with the Turkish Attorneys' Act and the Professional Rules of the Union of Turkish Bar Associations, we keep all information shared with us confidential as a professional secret.
