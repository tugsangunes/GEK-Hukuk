About Us

In Adana since 2014

GEK Hukuk Law Office was founded in 2014 by attorney Tuğsan Güneş. The firm provides advisory and litigation services to individuals and legal entities. We carry out our work within the framework of the Turkish Attorneys' Act and the Professional Rules of the Union of Turkish Bar Associations.

The firm's name comes from the initials of our lawyers' surnames: Güneş, Erdil, Karakaya.

Meetings and correspondence are conducted in Turkish or English.

About the firm

Practice Areas

Areas in which we provide advice and handle litigation

  • Corporate Law

    Legal transactions arising throughout a company's life, from incorporation to liquidation.

  • Commercial Law

    Contracts and disputes arising from relations between merchants and from commercial transactions.

  • Corporate Advisory

    Ongoing legal support for the day-to-day operations of companies.

  • Compliance

    Assessments and arrangements that help companies operate in line with applicable legislation.

  • Real Estate Law

    Transactions and disputes relating to the acquisition, use and transfer of immovable property.

  • Employment Law

    Establishment, conduct and termination of the employment relationship between employer and employee.

  • Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law

    Collection of receivables and proceedings concerning the debtor's assets.

  • Divorce and Family Law

    Legal matters arising from marriage and family relationships.

  • Inheritance Law

    Dispositions upon death and transactions and lawsuits concerning the division of an estate.

  • Intellectual Property Law

    Protection of rights arising from trademarks, patents, designs and authorship.

  • Representation in Mediation and Conciliation

    Representing parties as their attorney in mediation and conciliation proceedings.

How we work

How a matter progresses with us

  1. 01

    Initial meeting

    We listen to your matter and review the documents and information together.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    We explain the legal situation, the available options and their possible consequences clearly.

  3. 03

    Roadmap

    We prepare a written plan covering the process, time limits and the steps required.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    We keep you regularly informed at every stage of the matter.
