Practice Area

Real Estate Law

Transactions and disputes relating to the acquisition, use and transfer of immovable property.

Real estate law covers transactions and disputes concerning the ownership, use, lease and transfer of immovable property. In property sales, the official form requirement and the land registry records are decisive.

We provide advice and handle litigation concerning the cancellation and registration of title, leases, eviction and rent determination, construction agreements in exchange for land share and the partition of co-owned property.

Main topics

  • Title deed transactions and actions for cancellation and registration of title
  • Lease agreements, eviction and rent determination
  • Construction agreements in exchange for land share
  • Partition of co-owned property
  • Condominium disputes
