Privacy Notice (KVKK)

Last updated: October 2026

This is an English translation provided for information purposes. In case of any discrepancy, the Turkish version prevails.

This notice has been prepared pursuant to Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles to Be Followed in Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform, in order to inform persons who visit the gekhukuk.com website and contact us through it.

Clients who establish an attorney-client relationship with our firm are informed separately about the processing of their personal data when the power of attorney relationship is established.

1. Data controller

The attorneys at our firm are joint data controllers with respect to the personal data covered by this notice.

2. Personal data processed

  • Identity and contact data: Your name, surname and e-mail address provided through the contact form or by e-mail; your telephone number if you contact us by phone.
  • Content of your request: The information you include in your message.
  • Transaction security data: Records kept by the hosting provider during your visit to the site, such as IP address, date and time and browser information.

Please do not share sensitive personal data such as health data or criminal convictions, or detailed information about your legal dispute, through the contact form or by e-mail. Such information is obtained separately and confidentially during a meeting.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Receiving and responding to your contact and information requests,
  • Scheduling a meeting if you request one, and a preliminary assessment for establishing an attorney-client relationship,
  • Ensuring the security of the website,
  • Fulfilling obligations arising from legislation and responding to requests from competent authorities.

4. Method of collection and legal grounds

Your personal data are collected electronically, partly by automated means, through the contact form on the website, by e-mail and by telephone. The data are processed on the following legal grounds set out in Article 5(2) of the KVKK:

  • (c) Processing is directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract: assessing your requests concerning legal services,
  • (ç) Processing is necessary for the data controller to fulfil its legal obligations,
  • (e) Processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or protection of a right,
  • (f) Processing is necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller, provided that it does not harm the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject: responding to requests and ensuring site security.

As these legal grounds exist, your explicit consent is not separately obtained for your contact requests.

5. Transfer of personal data

Your personal data may be transferred, limited to the purposes above and in accordance with Articles 8 and 9 of the KVKK, to:

  • Competent public authorities and judicial bodies upon request,
  • Our business partners providing hosting and e-mail services for the website.

Transfer abroad: As the servers of our hosting provider GoDaddy and our e-mail provider Google are located abroad (mainly in the USA), personal data within the scope of your contact requests are transferred abroad for the provision of these services.

WhatsApp: If you choose to write to us via WhatsApp, your messages are transmitted through the infrastructure of WhatsApp Ireland Limited (Meta), which provides the WhatsApp service, and are subject to that company's own privacy terms. We recommend that you do not share the details of your dispute or sensitive personal data through this channel and use it only to make an appointment.

Site assistant: The questions you write to the assistant on the site are sent to the servers of our artificial intelligence provider Anthropic, PBC (USA) in order to generate answers. These messages are not stored by our firm; to prevent misuse, only an irreversible hash derived from your IP address is kept on our server for up to one day. We kindly ask you not to write your name, contact details, the details of your dispute or sensitive personal data to the assistant.

6. Retention period

Personal data processed in connection with contact requests are retained for 5 years after the request is concluded; if the request turns into a power of attorney relationship, the periods set out in the relevant legislation apply. At the end of the period, the data are deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to apply to our firm to:

  • Learn whether your personal data are processed,
  • Request information if they have been processed,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data are used in accordance with that purpose,
  • Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data are transferred,
  • Request rectification if the data are incomplete or inaccurate,
  • Request erasure or destruction of the data under the conditions set out in Article 7 of the KVKK,
  • Request that rectification, erasure and destruction be notified to third parties to whom the data have been transferred,
  • Object to a result to your detriment arising from the analysis of the processed data exclusively by automated systems,
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

8. How to apply

You may submit your requests in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, in writing together with information identifying you, by hand or by post to the address above, or by e-mail to info@gekhukuk.com from an e-mail address registered with our firm. Applications are concluded free of charge as soon as possible according to the nature of the request and within thirty days at the latest.
