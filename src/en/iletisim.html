Frequently asked questions

Before your first meeting

How can I make an appointment?

You can make an appointment by calling our office on weekdays between 09:00 and 17:00 (Türkiye time) or by writing to us using the form on this page.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Bringing the contracts, title deeds, notifications, correspondence and documents from courts or enforcement offices relating to your matter helps us assess the situation more quickly. In addition to your documents, a short timeline of events is also useful.

Will the information I share be kept confidential?

Yes. Under the Turkish Attorneys' Act, lawyers are obliged to keep confidential the information they learn in the course of their professional activities. Your personal data are processed as described in our Privacy Notice.

How are attorney's fees determined?

Fees are agreed with the client according to the nature and scope of the work and set out in a written agreement. Fees may not be lower than the Minimum Attorney Fee Tariff published by the Union of Turkish Bar Associations.

In which languages do you work?

We conduct meetings, correspondence and contract work in Turkish and English. Our website is also available in other languages for information purposes only.
