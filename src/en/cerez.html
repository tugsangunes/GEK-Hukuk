Cookie Policy

This website does not use cookies itself. All files, including fonts, are loaded from the site's own server; there are no third-party services for visitor tracking or advertising.

Map

The map on the contact page is not loaded until you press the "Show map" button. When you press it, the map is loaded from Google Maps (Google LLC); connection data such as your IP address are then transmitted to Google, and Google may place its own cookies in your browser. For information on how these data are processed, please see Google's privacy policy. You can also view our address without loading the map by using the "Open in Google Maps" link.
